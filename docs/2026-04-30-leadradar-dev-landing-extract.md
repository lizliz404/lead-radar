## Strategic read

Observed overlap with our project:

- Same/similar name: LeadRadar / Lead Radar.
- Same surface category: Reddit demand/lead monitoring.
- Same user: founders / B2B SaaS builders.
- Same evidence source: Reddit posts.
- Same mechanics: keywords, subreddit scanning, AI intent scoring, lead feed, alerts, reply/action loop.
- Same promise direction: find customers earlier and filter noise.

Important difference:

- leadradar.dev is positioned as self-serve Reddit lead-monitoring SaaS with free plan + $19/$39 subscriptions.
- Our current positioning after the latest change is paid research preview: market brief → source-linked evidence memo → human review → next action. That is the only defensible distinction right now. If we drift back toward dashboard/alerts/lead feed, we become a weaker clone.

Conclusion:

This is a red-ocean need with low obvious moat. The near-term path should not be “build the same SaaS but prettier.” The path should be to validate paid usefulness through manually reviewed research reports, specific niches, and proof that the artifact saves time or creates conversations/revenue.
